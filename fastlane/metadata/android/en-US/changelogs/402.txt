Mostly fixes for syncing, caching and composing:
- Trashing a conversation moves all its messages, and it no longer comes back after a sync.
- Drafts in a conversation can be edited and deleted.
- Replies open without waiting for the network.
- Fewer and faster requests: one sync for all accounts, and mail in view is loaded ahead on Wi-Fi.
- Updates keep the mail cache instead of rebuilding it.
