<b>This app requires a self-hosted plMail server.</b> It does not connect to mail providers by itself: it signs in to a plMail installation that you, or someone you trust, runs. Without one the app can do nothing. plMail is free software (AGPL-3.0), and setting up the server is described at github.com/karatektus/pl_mail

The server connects to the mailboxes you already have (IMAP, Gmail, Outlook) and keeps them in one inbox. This app brings that inbox to your phone.

The Android app is in beta: it is used every day, but expect rough edges, and please report what you find on GitHub.

<b>Every account in one inbox</b>
Read, search, label and file mail from all your accounts side by side, in conversations. Swipe between conversations, and reply, forward or write new mail with your signatures and sending aliases.

<b>A calendar beside the mail</b>
The week and month views from your server, synced with Google, Microsoft and CalDAV calendars, available as a separate launcher icon if you want one.

<b>New mail as it arrives</b>
Notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging, using your own server's Firebase project, or through any UnifiedPush distributor if you would rather not use Google.

<b>Encrypted mail</b>
Read and send OpenPGP and S/MIME encrypted mail. Your keys stay on the device, optionally protected by the screen lock.

<b>Built for servers at home</b>
Pair in seconds by scanning a QR code from your server's settings. LAN host names, Tailscale and private certificate authorities all work. Mail is cached on the device, so the app stays usable while your NAS restarts.

<b>No ads, no tracking</b>
plMail has no advertising and no tracking of its own. Your mail goes between your phone and your own server, nowhere else.

The same app is free on GitHub. Buying it here pays for automatic updates through Google Play and supports the project.
